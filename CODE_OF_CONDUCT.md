# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Kepler contributors and maintainers commit to a safe, respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community. Participation must remain free from harassment and discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, actionable, and respectful feedback.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and differing experience levels.
- Focus decisions on the project's users, security, and maintainability.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or sustained disruption
- Discriminatory, sexualized, insulting, or demeaning language or imagery
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Deliberate intimidation or retaliation
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered unsafe or unprofessional

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, chat spaces, events, and public representation of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `harisrini21@gmail.com`. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy, investigate promptly, and apply proportionate action. Possible actions include correction, warning, temporary exclusion, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

The BDFL is the final escalation point. A person involved in a report must not decide its outcome. When the BDFL is involved, an uninvolved maintainer handles the report.
