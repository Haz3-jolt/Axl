# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Kepler should be a safe, respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community. Harassment and discrimination are not welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work without attacking the person.
- Give specific, useful, and respectful feedback.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy, personal boundaries, and different levels of experience.
- Put user safety and long-term maintainability ahead of winning an argument.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or sustained disruption
- Discriminatory, sexualized, insulting, or demeaning language or imagery
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Intimidation or retaliation
- Conduct that a reasonable person would consider unsafe or unprofessional

## Scope

This policy applies to repositories, issue trackers, reviews, chat spaces, events, and public representation of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `harisrini21@gmail.com`. Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy, investigate promptly, and respond in proportion to the conduct. Responses may include a correction, warning, temporary exclusion, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

The BDFL is the final escalation point. Anyone involved in a report must not decide its outcome. If the BDFL is involved, an uninvolved maintainer handles the report.
